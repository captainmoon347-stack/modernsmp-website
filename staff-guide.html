🔥 Elite Staff (Application Closed)

  • 👑 Owner
    • Complete ownership of the network.
    • Final decisions on staff, punishments and server management.
    • Manages all departments and future updates.

  • 🤝 Co-Owner
    • Assists the Owner.
    • Manages staff and server operations.
    • Can make important decisions when the Owner is unavailable.

  • 🛡️ Admin
    • Supervises all staff members.
    • Handles serious reports and appeals.
    • Organizes events and manages moderators.

  • 💻 Developer
    • Develops Discord bots and website features.
    • Configures Minecraft plugins.
    • Fixes bugs and maintains the network.

  • ⭐ Head Moderator
    • Leads the moderation team.
    • Reviews punishments and staff reports.
    • Trains new moderators.

  • 📋 Head Manager
    • Manages community projects.
    • Oversees managers and event planning.
    • Ensures smooth server operations.

❌ Elite Staff positions cannot be applied for directly. Promotion is based on trust, activity and performance.

🛡️ Secondary Staff (Applications Open)

  • 🆘 Helper
    • Helps members with questions.
    • Reports rule breakers.
    • Assists moderators.

  • 🛡️ Moderator
    • Warns and times out rule breakers.
    • Keeps chats clean.
    • Handles basic reports.

  • 📂 Manager
    • Manages events and small staff teams.
    • Helps organize the community.

  • 🌍 Community Manager ⭐
    • Highest Secondary Staff Role.
    • Manages the community.
    • Gives feedback to Elite Staff.
    • Can be promoted to Elite Staff.

  • 🎉 Event Manager
    • Hosts Minecraft, Roblox and Among Us events.
    • Plans tournaments and giveaways.

  • 🤖 Bot Manager
    • Configures Discord bots.
    • Maintains automod, tickets and logging.

  • 🧪 Quality Tester
    • Tests new features.
    • Reports bugs and improvements.

  • 📦 Resource Supplier
    • Provides maps, plugins, assets and server resources.

  • 💎 VIP Booster
    • Supports the community through boosts and activity.
    • Helps promote the server.

📈 Promotion Path

Helper → Moderator → Manager → Community Manager ⭐ → Elite Staff

✅ Public applications are available only for Secondary Staff roles. Outstanding staff members may be promoted to the Elite Staff Team based on performance, activity and trust.
